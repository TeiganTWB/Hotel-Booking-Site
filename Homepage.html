A Tradition of Excellence

Established in 1985, Crimson Luxe stands as a beacon of sophisticated hospitality in the heart of the city. Our commitment to excellence is reflected in every aspect of our service, from our elegantly designed rooms and suites to our world-class dining experiences.

Whether you're traveling for business or pleasure, our dedicated staff is here to ensure your stay exceeds all expectations. We pride ourselves on attention to detail and personalized service that keeps our guests returning year after year.

Our Journey

Through Time

Discover the rich heritage and milestones that have shaped Crimson Luxe into what it is today

The Beginning

Crimson Luxe was established with a vision to create a luxury hospitality experience unlike any other in the region.
